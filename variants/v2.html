03 · the anchor

A number that failed, then reappeared

July 2025: a Porsche 918 Spyder from this seller runs to $2,305,918 and does not meet reserve — no sale, but the number is public. Ten weeks later a second 918 from the same seller sells for $2,301,000 — within 0.2% of the failed number.

how often does that happen anywhere? · the market-wide null

Across every ≥$500k reserve-not-met lot market-wide (Apr 7 – Aug 1 2026), there were 463 chances for a later comparable sale to land within ±2% of the failed number. 0 did. The 918 pair, plotted on the same axis, sits alone inside the band.

The innocent reading: two owners of the same hypercar can honestly hold the same view of its value, and a failed number can simply have been right. That is why the pair is judged against the market’s own base rate — below — rather than against zero.
