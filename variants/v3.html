Ten lives, one seller.

A structural reading of one auction cohort: how much of each bidder account's lifetime bends toward a single consignor, and the exact relay by which eighteen bids carried a GT2 RS from $348,340 to $1,000,000. Every figure below is computed from public bid ladders. Pattern is not proof — each section carries its innocent explanation alongside the finding.

05 · Verdict board

Ten strategies, ten honest statuses

The full strategy taxonomy scored against this dataset. PROVEN is reserved for effects that survive base-rate and multiple-comparison controls; SCREENS are candidate lists, not findings; INDETERMINATE outcomes are stated as such. Statements grade auctions and prices — never people.

A1 · A2REVIEW LEADS

Laddering / chandelier pattern

The 18-bid GT2 RS walk is carried mostly by zero-purchase accounts; chandelier bidding up to reserve is lawful in many venues, so pattern alone cannot separate the twins.

innocent twin Enthusiasts bid and lose for years; zero completed purchases is the platform base case.

B3PROVEN TAIL

Poison bid / winner default

Sold-then-relisted signature: a measured 7.79× default-rate multiple in the flagged tail (p = 6.5e-7). A statement about prices, not persons.

innocent twin Genuine financing collapse or failed post-sale inspection produces single instances — not a stable tail.

A4 · C4POSSIBLE

Reserve protection

GT2 RS reserve publicly removed late, after the walk-up, converting a standing bid into a recorded comp.

innocent twin A seller capitulating to a real market below expectation shows the opposite price direction.

A3POSSIBLE

Comp anchoring (failed number → later sale)

Same-seller repeats cluster 2.4–5.0× above the cross-seller base rate; the 918 pair sits within 0.2%.

innocent twin A specialist with consistent pricing also produces same-seller clustering; a rising market grows into old numbers.

—UNRESOLVED

Account rotation

Whether thin accounts are retired and replaced cannot be resolved from bid ladders alone; requires longitudinal identity data we do not hold.

innocent twin Normal member churn produces the same surface pattern.

C141 SCREENS

Reciprocal ping-pong (A buys B, B buys A)

41 account pairs pass the reciprocity screen; screens are candidates for review, not findings.

innocent twin Dealers genuinely trade with each other constantly in this market.

A1 signatureNAMED LEADS

Seller-affinity concentration

4 of the 10 GT2 RS ladder-adjacent accounts put ≥10% of their lifetime bids on this one seller; one puts 100%.

innocent twin Specialist taste also produces convergence — a marque devotee follows the consignor with the best cars.

A6655 SCREENS

Collusive partitioning (don't-bid-against-me)

655 pairs pass the non-overlap screen at population scale; expected false-positive volume is high — triage only.

innocent twin Taste segmentation: two collectors who want different cars never collide.

B4 · B5TRANSACTION FEATURE

Last-minute pressure / sniping

Late bidding is a legitimate, rational strategy; the literature subtracts it from shill scores and so do we.

innocent twin This row exists to say so explicitly: snipers are not a signal.

—1 WITHDRAWAL

Provenance misstatement

One listing withdrawn after the seller self-reported a description issue — the disclosure system working as designed.

innocent twin Self-correction is evidence of process, not of pattern.
