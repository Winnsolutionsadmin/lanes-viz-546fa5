Four ways to draw the same case

Variant 2

Variant 3

Convergence / flow

Ribbons of ten lives bending into one seller node. Relay-baton ladder: who handed the price to whom, counter ticking to $1M.
Variant 4

The editorial braid

Threads knotting at the four story dates, magazine margin notes. Annotated real-time staircase. The journalist-ready long read.
