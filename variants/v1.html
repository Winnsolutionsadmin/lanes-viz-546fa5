A number that failed to transact, realized at 0.2% ten weeks later

One timeline, July to October 2025. A 918 Spyder bid to $2,305,918 — reserve not met, so the number never transacted — and a different 918 from the same seller selling for $2,301,000 ten weeks later. Below it: the 463 comparable ≥$500k failed-number opportunities in four months of the full market, placed by how far the later sale landed from the failed number. 0 of 463 landed within ±2%.

The innocent reading, inline: a seller who relists similar inventory can genuinely realize close to a prior high bid, and a rising market grows into failed numbers. The base-rate strip is the control for that story: market-wide, at this price tier, the closest any analog got was 7.8% — the 0.2% landing is an outlier, not an impossibility.
